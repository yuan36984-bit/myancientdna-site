Your DNA Story Matches: Ancient Egyptian

In July 2025, researchers recovered the first whole genome from Egypt's Old Kingdom — a man buried at Nuwayrat roughly 4,500–4,800 years ago, during the era of the pyramid builders. His ancestry was predominantly North African, with a notable share tracing to ancient Mesopotamia — an early genetic signal of contact between Egypt and the Fertile Crescent.
