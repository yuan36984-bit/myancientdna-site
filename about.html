About MyAncientDNA.com

MyAncientDNA.com is an independent, reader-supported resource that explains ancient DNA ancestry testing in plain language — what it is, how it works, and how the major consumer DNA platforms compare when it comes to deep-time ancestry.

We are not a DNA testing company. We don't process DNA samples, and we don't have access to any reader's genetic data. Our content is based on publicly available information, published research, and each provider's own documentation, reviewed and summarized independently.

Independence and affiliation

MyAncientDNA.com is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to MyHeritage, Ancestry, 23andMe, Illustrative DNA, or any other DNA testing company mentioned on this site. Company and product names are used solely to identify and discuss the services in question.

How the site is funded

This site is supported by on-page advertising (including Google AdSense). Advertising does not influence the content or conclusions of our guides and reviews.

Corrections and feedback

DNA testing products and pricing change frequently. If you spot something on this site that's out of date, please get in touch via our contact page.
