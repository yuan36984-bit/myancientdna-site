MyHeritage DNA Review

Independent review · Not affiliated with MyHeritage · Updated 2026

This is an independent, unofficial review. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by MyHeritage. Always confirm current pricing and features on MyHeritage's own site before purchasing.

MyHeritage has offered consumer DNA testing since 2016, alongside its long-running family-tree and genealogy record platform. The DNA product centers on two things: an ethnicity estimate that breaks your ancestry into regional percentages, and DNA Matches, which connects you with other MyHeritage users who share detectable segments of DNA with you — useful for finding living relatives and verifying a family tree.

The core kit

Like other major providers, MyHeritage DNA uses a simple cheek-swab sample processed on a genotyping chip. You can also upload raw DNA data from AncestryDNA, 23andMe, or another provider directly to MyHeritage instead of buying a new kit, which unlocks the ethnicity estimate and matching features (with some advanced tools reserved for subscribers) — see our step-by-step testing guide for exactly how that upload process works.

Ethnicity Estimate

MyHeritage's Ethnicity Estimate breaks your ancestry into regional percentages based on comparison to a reference panel of modern populations with documented multi-generation roots in specific regions. As with any consumer ethnicity estimate — from any company — results are statistical estimates that can shift modestly as reference panels are updated, not a fixed, permanent figure.

DNA Matches and genealogy tools

Because MyHeritage's core business is genealogy records (historical documents, family trees, census data), its DNA Matches feature integrates directly with that ecosystem — shared matches can be cross-referenced against family tree data and historical records in ways that are less built-out on some DNA-only competitors.

Subscription tiers

MyHeritage separates its record/genealogy subscription from DNA-specific features, and its higher tiers (Complete and Omni) unlock additional tools, including the Ancient Origins feature covered in our separate review. Free accounts get basic ethnicity results and limited matching; deeper tools require a paid plan. Check MyHeritage's current pricing page for exact tier costs, as these are updated periodically. For background on what "ancient DNA" actually means before you decide whether it's worth the upgrade, see our plain-English explainer.

How it compares

MyHeritage tends to be strongest for genealogy record research and users already invested in family-tree building, and it's currently the most prominent mainstream option for ancient-DNA population comparisons. AncestryDNA has the larger user base and match database in several regions, which matters for finding relatives. 23andMe has historically emphasized granularity in its modern ethnicity breakdown and additional trait/health reports. See the full breakdown in our DNA ancestry services comparison, or jump straight to our rankings for the most accurate DNA test and the cheapest DNA test if that's your main deciding factor.

Mobile app and accessibility

MyHeritage offers a mobile app (iOS and Android) that mirrors most of the core web features — ethnicity results, DNA Matches, family tree browsing, and record hints. Notifications for new DNA matches or record hints typically come through the app, which is useful for users who don't check the web dashboard regularly. If you're specifically looking for the Ancient Origins panel, see our walkthrough of where it lives in the dashboard.

Customer support

Support is available through a help center with searchable articles, plus email-based ticket support. Paid subscribers on higher tiers generally get faster response times, though MyHeritage doesn't typically offer live phone or chat support in the way some larger consumer platforms do — factor this in if you expect to need hands-on help interpreting results.

International availability

MyHeritage is based in Israel and has historically had strong adoption across Europe, given the company's origins as a genealogy records aggregator with deep European historical archives — it's also a frequently recommended platform for researching Ashkenazi Jewish ancestry specifically, given that focus. DNA kits ship internationally to most countries, though shipping times and customs handling vary by region — check current shipping availability for your country on MyHeritage's site before ordering.
