What "Polynesian ancestry" actually means in your results

Polynesian ancestry traces to a mixture of Austronesian ancestry (originating near Taiwan) and Papuan-related ancestry, acquired through contact in the Bismarck Archipelago before the wider Pacific expansion. See our full Polynesian & Pacific Islander ancestry guide for the details.

Don't skip the free option

If you've already tested elsewhere, some providers let you upload existing raw DNA data at low or no cost rather than buying a second full kit. See our step-by-step testing guide for how that process generally works.

FAQ

Why is Polynesian ancestry harder to pin down precisely than other categories?

Reference panels for remote Pacific populations have historically been smaller than for more heavily studied world regions, given the practical challenges of sampling geographically dispersed island populations. This affects precision across all providers, not just one.

Does a Polynesian ancestry result also mean I have Denisovan ancestry?

Not necessarily as a package, but Papuan and some other Pacific populations carry among the highest known levels of Denisovan ancestry in the world, so the two can be related. See our Denisovan DNA explainer for details.
