Ancient DNA Ancestry: Frequently Asked Questions

Updated 2026

What is ancient DNA ancestry testing?

It compares your genetic data to DNA recovered from archaeological remains dated to specific historical periods, showing which sampled ancient populations your DNA most closely resembles — as opposed to a standard ethnicity estimate, which compares you to modern reference populations. See our full explainer.

Do I need a new DNA test to see ancient origins results?

Usually not. Ancient-origins features are typically calculated from the same raw DNA data used for your standard ethnicity estimate, so if you've already tested or uploaded raw data to a provider offering the feature, no new sample is required. See our testing guide for the full step-by-step process either way.

How accurate are ancient DNA ancestry results?

Results are statistical estimates based on comparison to available ancient DNA reference samples. They can shift over time as reference panels grow, and they describe population-level resemblance rather than a documented, unbroken family lineage.

Is ancient DNA testing free?

It depends on the provider. Some ancient-origins features, such as MyHeritage's Ancient Origins, are gated behind a premium subscription tier rather than included with a free account or a one-time DNA kit purchase. See our pricing breakdown.

How is ancient DNA testing different from a regular ethnicity estimate?

A regular ethnicity estimate compares your DNA to modern populations with documented family history in a region, typically covering the last few hundred years. Ancient DNA testing compares your DNA directly to archaeological samples that can be thousands of years old.

Is my DNA data safe if I use an ancient origins feature?

Ancient-origins analysis uses the same raw DNA data as other features on a platform, so the same privacy considerations apply. Review the provider's privacy policy for data retention, third-party research use, and deletion options before testing or uploading.

Which company offers ancient DNA ancestry testing?

As of 2026, MyHeritage is the most prominent mainstream provider offering a dedicated ancient-population comparison feature (Ancient Origins), built in partnership with Illustrative DNA. See our full comparison of major providers.

How far back can ancient DNA ancestry testing go?

MyHeritage's Ancient Origins compares your DNA to archaeological samples spanning roughly the last 10,000 years, from the Neolithic period through the late Middle Ages, depending on which ancient reference populations are available for comparison. See our ancient DNA explainer for more on the underlying science.

How long does it take to get ancient DNA results?

If you already have DNA data on file with a provider, ancient-origins results are typically available within minutes of enabling the feature. If you're testing for the first time, add the usual multi-week processing time for a new DNA kit before any results, ancient or modern, become available. Once it's ready, see our walkthrough of where to find it in your dashboard.

Can I delete my ancient DNA results and raw data?

Most providers allow you to delete uploaded raw DNA data and associated reports from your account settings. Deletion policies and retention periods vary by company, so check the specific provider's privacy policy or help center for their current process — we cover MyHeritage's specific deletion path in our Ancient Origins review.
