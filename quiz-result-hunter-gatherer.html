Your DNA Story Matches: Hunter-Gatherer

Long before farming reached Europe around 6000 BCE, hunter-gatherer groups had already lived across the continent for tens of thousands of years. One of the best-known examples is Cheddar Man, a roughly 10,000-year-old skeleton found in Somerset, England — ancient-DNA analysis by London's Natural History Museum found he likely had dark skin and blue eyes, a combination that reshaped assumptions about what early Europeans looked like.
