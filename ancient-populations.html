Ancient Populations Guide

Directory · Updated 2026

Ancient-origins panels compare your DNA against a set of archaeological reference populations, each associated with a specific place, time period, and body of published research. This page is a directory of every ancient population covered on this site — pick the one that shows up in your results to see what it actually means.

Western Eurasia & the Bronze Age world

Bronze Age Steppe

The Yamnaya-associated pastoralists whose westward migration around 5,000 years ago reshaped European ancestry.

Hunter-Gatherer

Europe's deepest resident ancestry, predating farming by tens of thousands of years.

Early European Farmer
